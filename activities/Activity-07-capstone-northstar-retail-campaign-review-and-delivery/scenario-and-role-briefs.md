## Scenario

Northstar Home, a Singapore lifestyle retailer, needs a six-image launch set for web, social media, and an in-store screen. Stakeholders disagree about mood, two source images have uncertain licence records, one portrait contains location metadata, and the marketing lead wants a shareable review album by 3:15 pm. You must manage the brief, editing, evaluation, collaboration, IP checks, and delivery trail.
