# Source Coverage - v12.0

Release date: 19 August 2026

The 138-slide v10 legacy deck is the coverage floor. The v12.0 release preserves its three official domains while replacing procedural slide walls with visual concepts and moving detailed workflows into the Learner Guide and seven individual activity folders.

## Legacy coverage retained

- Topic 1: Lightroom ecosystem, workspace, import, mobile capture, albums, folders, keywords, people, flags, ratings, filters, search, metadata and capture-time correction.
- Topic 2: design principles, histogram/clipping, profiles, tone, curve, colour, crop/geometry, effects, detail, optics, presets, masking, HDR, panorama and enhancement.
- Topic 3: intellectual property, shared albums, collaboration, export, watermarking and local storage.

## Expanded current coverage

- Non-destructive versioning, stakeholder briefs, colour-aware workspace, controlled experimentation and evidence-based culling.
- Current Lightroom/Classic/web/mobile positioning, AI-assisted masking, natural Feather/Edge refinement, Generative Remove, distraction removal, Denoise, AI Sharpen, Generative Upscale and Generative Expand with human quality control.
- Rights/consent, provenance, privacy, least-privilege sharing, destination-specific export, quality assurance, delivery manifests and audit-ready archives.
