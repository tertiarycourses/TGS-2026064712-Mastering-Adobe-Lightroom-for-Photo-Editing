## About the course

Learners explore digital-imagery techniques in Adobe Lightroom, evaluate digital-imaging capabilities against design principles, and discuss the image-production process, including intellectual-property considerations, through seven connected workplace activities.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1:** Explore ideas and techniques of digital imagery and improve on the techniques for image production using Lightroom.
- **LO2:** Evaluate the capabilities of digital imaging techniques with regard to the theoretical contexts and principle of design.
- **LO3:** Discuss the image production process with others with intellectual property considerations.

## Topics covered

1. Get Started on Digital Imagery Techniques Using Lightroom
2. Evaluate Digital Imaging Capabilities using Lightroom
3. Process of Digital Image Production

## Skills practised

- Translate stakeholder intent into observable image-editing criteria.
- Build a colour-aware, non-destructive Lightroom workflow.
- Organise, cull, retrieve, and version image assets reliably.
- Apply tone, colour, geometry, detail, masking, retouching, and AI-assisted enhancement with human quality control.
- Evaluate edits through design principles, technical evidence, authenticity, rights, privacy, and output fitness.
- Review, share, export, deliver, and archive a traceable production package.

## Learning design

The 141-slide trainer deck is deliberately concept-led and highly visual. It uses editable PowerPoint frameworks, an AI-generated topic cover, and curated visuals imported from the original trainer deck. Detailed click-by-click procedures are kept in the Learner Guide and activity packs.

The seven connected workplace activities cover stakeholder briefing, workspace readiness, culling and metadata, global editing consistency, geometry and masking, ethical portrait retouching, AI-assisted image rescue, and a multi-channel campaign capstone.

## Distribution boundary

Candidate and answer-key assessment documents are maintained in controlled trainer storage and are intentionally excluded from this public repository, together with source references (`reference/`) and the Facilitator Guide and Assessment Plan. Learners download the course material from the LMS at https://lms-tms.tertiaryinfotech.com. The approved assessment design uses Oral Questioning and Practical Performance; this package does not contain a practice exam.
